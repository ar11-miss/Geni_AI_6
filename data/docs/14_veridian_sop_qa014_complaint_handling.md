# Veridian MedTech SOP-QA-014 – Complaint Handling and Adverse Event Reporting (Rev. F)

> Fictional example document created for this RAG demonstration. Veridian MedTech is not a real company.

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure defines how Veridian MedTech receives, documents, evaluates, investigates and closes complaints for all marketed products, including the InfusePro 300 infusion pump, and how reportability to FDA (21 CFR Part 803) and EU competent authorities (MDR Article 87) is determined. It implements ISO 13485:2016 clause 8.2.2 and the FDA QMSR.

## Responsibilities
- Any employee: forward any complaint information to the Complaint Unit.
- Complaint Coordinator (Complaint Unit): logs, triages and tracks complaints.
- Regulatory Affairs (RA): makes the reportability decision and submits eMDR and EU vigilance reports.
- Quality Engineering (QE): performs the investigation and root-cause analysis.
- Complaint Review Board (CRB): meets weekly on Tuesdays to review open complaints, trends and CAPA escalations.

## Complaint Intake
Any employee who receives information alleging a deficiency related to the identity, quality, durability, reliability, safety, effectiveness or performance of a device must forward it to the Complaint Unit within 1 business day. The Complaint Coordinator enters the complaint into the Veridian Quality Management System (VQMS) within 1 business day of receipt by the Complaint Unit. The date any Veridian employee first receives the information is recorded as the "awareness date" (Day 0) for regulatory reporting clocks. The Complaint Coordinator requests return of the involved device within 5 business days of intake.

## Reportability Assessment
Regulatory Affairs completes the initial MDR and vigilance reportability assessment within 2 business days of complaint entry. Any complaint involving a death or serious injury must be escalated by telephone and email to the VP of Quality and the Head of Regulatory Affairs within 24 hours of intake. Internal target: reportable 30-day MDRs are submitted no later than calendar day 25 after the awareness date to provide margin before the 30-day regulatory deadline; 5-day reports and EU 2-day reports are submitted on the timelines required by regulation.

## Investigation and Closure
Quality Engineering completes the investigation and root-cause analysis within 30 calendar days for non-reportable complaints and within 60 calendar days for reportable complaints. If a complaint is not investigated, QE documents the justification and the name of the person responsible for the decision. Complaints are closed by the Complaint Coordinator after QA approval in VQMS.

## Trending and CAPA Escalation
Complaints are trended monthly by product family and failure mode. A CAPA must be opened when any of the following occurs: three or more complaints with the same failure mode within a rolling 90-day period; any complaint involving a death or serious injury where a device malfunction is confirmed; or a statistically significant upward trend identified by the monthly trend analysis.

## Records
Complaint records are retained for the expected service life of the device plus 2 years, and in no case less than 7 years from the date of closure.
