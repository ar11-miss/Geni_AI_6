# Veridian MedTech InfusePro 300 Volumetric Infusion Pump – Instructions for Use (Excerpt)

> Fictional example document created for this RAG demonstration. Veridian MedTech and the InfusePro 300 are not real.

## Device Description and Intended Use
The InfusePro 300 is a large-volume, single-channel volumetric infusion pump intended for the controlled delivery of fluids, medications, blood and blood products via intravenous, intra-arterial, subcutaneous and epidural routes, in hospital and outpatient clinical settings, by trained healthcare professionals. In the United States it is a Class II device under product code FRN (21 CFR 880.5725). In the EU it is a Class IIb device under MDR Annex VIII Rule 12. Current software version: 4.2.1. The pump includes the DoseGuard drug library with hard limits (cannot be overridden) and soft limits (can be overridden after clinician confirmation).

## Technical Specifications
- Flow rate range: 0.1 to 999 mL/h (0.1 mL/h increments from 0.1 to 99.9 mL/h; 1 mL/h increments from 100 to 999 mL/h).
- Volume to be infused (VTBI): 0.1 to 9999 mL.
- Flow rate accuracy: ±5% under nominal conditions (per IEC 60601-2-24 test methods).
- KVO (Keep Vein Open) rate after VTBI complete: default 1.0 mL/h, configurable from 0.1 to 5.0 mL/h (or the programmed rate, if lower).
- Downstream occlusion pressure alarm thresholds: Low 300 mmHg, Medium 500 mmHg (default), High 800 mmHg.
- Air-in-line detection: single bubble of 50 µL or larger, or cumulative air of 1.0 mL within 15 minutes.
- Battery: rechargeable 7.4 V lithium-ion; operating time at least 8 hours at 25 mL/h from full charge; full recharge time 5 hours when connected to AC mains; battery pack replacement recommended every 24 months.
- Ingress protection: IPX2. Weight: 2.1 kg.

## Alarm and Error Codes
- E-101 Downstream occlusion: check the patient line for closed clamps, kinks or a clotted catheter; clear the cause and press RESUME.
- E-102 Upstream occlusion: check the container spike and upstream clamp; ensure the container is not empty.
- E-103 Air-in-line: stop the infusion, open the door, remove the air per hospital protocol, re-prime the set, and restart.
- E-201 Low battery: approximately 30 minutes of operation remain; connect to AC mains.
- E-202 Battery depleted: approximately 5 minutes remain before the pump stops; connect to AC mains immediately.
- E-301 Door open: close and latch the pump door.
- E-401 System fault: the pump has detected an internal error. Stop the infusion, remove the pump from service, label it "Do Not Use", and contact Veridian Technical Service. Do not attempt to restart the pump on a patient.

## Cleaning and Maintenance
Clean the external surfaces between patients with a soft cloth moistened with 70% isopropyl alcohol or a 0.5% sodium hypochlorite solution. Do not immerse the pump or spray liquid directly into the door mechanism or connectors, and do not autoclave. A preventive maintenance check, including flow accuracy and occlusion pressure verification, must be performed by qualified biomedical personnel every 12 months. The expected service life of the InfusePro 300 is 7 years.
