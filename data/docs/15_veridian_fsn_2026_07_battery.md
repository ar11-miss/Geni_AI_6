# Veridian MedTech Urgent Field Safety Notice FSN-2026-07 – InfusePro 300 Battery Gauge Issue

> Fictional example document created for this RAG demonstration. Veridian MedTech is not a real company.

## Affected Products
InfusePro 300 volumetric infusion pumps with serial numbers IP3-240001 through IP3-245500 running software version 4.2.0. Pumps already running software version 4.2.1 are not affected.

## Description of the Problem
Veridian has identified that on affected pumps the software battery gauge may overestimate the remaining battery charge when the actual remaining charge is below 10%. As a result, the E-201 Low Battery alarm may not occur, and the pump may stop infusing with only the E-202 Battery Depleted alarm and less than 1 minute of warning, instead of the specified approximately 5 minutes. An interruption of infusion of critical medications (for example vasopressors or inotropes) could lead to serious harm. Veridian has received 14 complaints related to this issue, including 2 reports of delayed therapy; no deaths have been reported.

## Actions to Be Taken by Users
1. Until the software update is installed, keep affected pumps connected to AC mains power whenever possible, especially during infusion of critical or life-sustaining medications.
2. Do not rely on the battery gauge display; during patient transport, use a fully charged pump and monitor the infusion continuously.
3. Complete and return the Customer Acknowledgement Form to Veridian within 10 days of receiving this notice, even if you no longer have affected pumps.
4. Forward this notice to all users within your organization who need to be aware of it.

## Actions Taken by Veridian
Veridian is providing software version 4.2.1, which corrects the battery gauge algorithm. Veridian Field Service will install the update on all affected pumps by 15 December 2026, with no charge to customers. This action has been reported to FDA as a correction under 21 CFR Part 806 and is being conducted as a voluntary Class II recall in the United States; it is being reported as a Field Safety Corrective Action to the EU competent authorities. Contact Veridian Technical Service at the number on your pump label for questions.
